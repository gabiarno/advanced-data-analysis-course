# Certificate of attendance — content template

**Confirm with the organiser before producing these.** LPC Training may issue certificates themselves, under their own branding and wording. If they do, use theirs and discard this. This template exists so that the wording is ready if it falls to the instructor.

Print on A4 landscape, one per participant. Names must be spelled from the organiser's official participant list, not from a sign-in sheet — check the spelling of every name with the organiser before printing.

## Notes

- **Keep the final disclaimer.** The course is formatively assessed over five days; describing it as a competency certification would not be accurate, and the participants' employers may rely on the wording.
- Adjust the hours figure if the contract settles on 25 contact hours rather than 22.5.
- "Attended" is correct for participants who were present. Do not issue a certificate to anyone who did not attend, even if their name is on the list.
- Print after the course, not before — attendance is the thing being certified.
