# Review of the merged curriculum revision

Reviewed baseline: commit `3ee2124cb19397b16152f4b5d1142368032d6025`, merged curriculum and delivery pack.

## Preserved

The participant handbook, method cards, capstone, instructor question bank, Spanish orientation, technical notebooks, slide files and the review's distinction between implemented and proposed material. The capstone already assessed decisions without coding and remains the common project.

## Corrected in this adaptation

- Keyboard-driver rotation did not provide a workable route when nobody codes. A complete paper workbook and separate answers now supply the five-day core.
- The pre-course pack required software even for non-programmers and promised alternative environments not yet verified. Core participation now requires only paper and pen; coding setup is optional.
- The handbook's worked briefing used 63 and 53 channel order counts. It now distinguishes 60 Store and 54 Online priced orders from 61 and 56 valid orders, matching the recorded course data.
- The curriculum review's addition estimates sum to 180 minutes, not 150. They must replace existing blocks rather than extend a five-hour day silently.
- Five interpreted group presentations now receive 35 minutes, with written individual submissions from all 20 learners. The 20-minute final assessment has a separate no-code rubric.
- The programme now distinguishes analytical judgement from independent model implementation and makes the default no-code outcomes explicit.

Checked the hand-calculation cases, confusion-matrix costs, schedule totals and the generated handbook build. Embedded links to included handbook sections use internal anchors. The generated handbook includes the participant workbook, not the instructor answer key.

No numerical notebook implementation was changed or rerun for this adaptation. Recorded outputs are reused and new hand-calculation cases are clearly separate. Classroom timing and interpretation pace still need rehearsal.
