# Final practical assessment — 20 minutes plus group reporting

Work in pairs, using the notebooks and saved results. The task assesses interpretation and evaluation choices, not typing speed. Use the interpreter for instructions and reporting. Each participant should explain at least one part.

## Scenario

An operations manager wants to forecast next week's demand, identify delayed routes, categorise support messages and share synthetic service data. The manager claims: “Our toy text model scored 100%, our generator matched the mean, and Spark ran locally, so we are ready for production.”

## Deliverable

Write a one-page recommendation answering:

1. Choose a suitable method for demand, delayed-route classification and message categorisation. State the output of each. (4 minutes)
2. Give an appropriate evaluation split and a baseline for demand and delayed routes. (4 minutes)
3. Interpret two numerical results from the course, including one limitation for each. (4 minutes)
4. Correct the manager's three claims. (4 minutes)
5. Propose one concrete next step before deployment. (4 minutes)

Combine pairs into five groups for the existing 25-minute reporting slot. Each group has roughly two minutes to speak and three minutes for interpretation and feedback.

Instructor rubric and model response are in FINAL_PRACTICAL_KEY.md. This short exercise is not a professional competency certification.
