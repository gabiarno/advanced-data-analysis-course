# Entry diagnostic — 10 minutes

This is a placement discussion, not a graded exam. Ask about previous tools and objectives as well as these questions. Share the questions separately from the key below if needed.

1. What is the result of `len([4, 7, 9])`? A: 2; B: 3; C: 20.
2. In a table of orders, what would one row usually represent? A: an order; B: every customer; C: a Python library.
3. A price is missing. What should you do first? A: set it to zero; B: remove the entire dataset; C: investigate its meaning and impact.
4. Which is less affected by one extremely large observation? A: median; B: mean; C: neither ever changes.
5. Sales and advertising move together. Does that prove advertising caused sales? A: yes; B: no.
6. Why hold out test data? A: to assess performance on unseen examples; B: to make the dataset smaller for no reason; C: to train twice.
7. Predicting a numerical delivery time is usually: A: regression; B: clustering; C: classification.
8. Should a forecast be trained on observations after the forecast date? A: yes; B: no.

## Instructor key

1 B; 2 A; 3 C; 4 A; 5 B; 6 A; 7 A; 8 B.

Questions 1–4 check foundational readiness; 5–8 indicate analytical/model-evaluation familiarity. Do not equate a total score with professional competence. If most learners struggle with 1–4, increase foundational support and discuss the scope with the organiser. If most already know all answers, ask about real modelling work before choosing advanced extensions.
